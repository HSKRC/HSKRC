A distinction essential to Nizari history

Eight Fatimid imam-caliphs, from al-Mahdi through al-Mustansir, form part of the historical shared Ismaili Imamat. After the disputed succession of 1094, the Nizari tradition recognised Nizar; later rulers in Egypt followed a different line. Nizar never ruled as the Fatimid caliph of Egypt.

Shared early Fatimid imam-caliphs · 909–1094

  1. 909–934

    al-Mahdi biʾllah

    Established the Fatimid state in North Africa.

  2. 934–946

    al-Qaʾim bi-Amr Allah

    Second imam-caliph; faced a major uprising.

  3. 946–953

    al-Mansur biʾllah

    Defeated Abu Yazid's revolt and founded al-Mansuriyya.

  4. 953–975

    al-Muʿizz li-Din Allah

    Directed expansion into Egypt and the foundation of Cairo.

  5. 975–996

    al-ʿAziz biʾllah

    Consolidated rule in Egypt; construction began on the al-Hakim Mosque.

  6. 996–1021

    al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah

    Founded Dar al-Ilm in 1005; al-Hakim Mosque was completed in his reign.

  7. 1021–1036

    al-Zahir li-Iʿzaz Din Allah

    Continued Cairo-centred Fatimid rule.

  8. 1036–1094

    al-Mustansir biʾllah

    His death was followed by the disputed 1094 succession.

Later Fatimid caliphs of Egypt · 1094–1171

These reign dates refer to the political history of Fatimid Egypt, not to the Nizari Imamat. The transition around 1130–1132 involved contested arrangements and should not be flattened into an uninterrupted succession.

  1. 1094–1101

    al-Mustaʿli biʾllah

    Egyptian Fatimid ruler recognised by the Mustaʿli branch.

  2. 1101–1130

    al-Amir bi-Ahkam Allah

    Al-Aqmar Mosque was commissioned during his reign.

  3. 1132–1149

    al-Hafiz li-Din Allah

    Later Egyptian ruler following an unsettled succession.

  4. 1149–1154

    al-Zafir bi-Amr Allah

    Later Fatimid caliph amid political upheavals.

  5. 1154–1160

    al-Faʾiz bi-Nasr Allah

    A child caliph in Egypt.

  6. 1160–1171

    al-ʿAdid li-Din Allah

    Last Fatimid caliph; rule ended in 1171.
