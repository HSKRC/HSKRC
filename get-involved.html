Contribution principles

Participation should strengthen accuracy, access, respect and responsible preservation.

Evidence first

Historical or research claims should be linked to identifiable sources whenever possible.

Respectful participation

Contributions should support constructive learning and avoid derogatory, abusive or discriminatory material.

Copyright-aware

Please only submit files or materials that you have the right to share. Bibliographic suggestions are welcome even when a full PDF cannot be hosted.

Transparent credit

Where appropriate, significant community or research contributions can be acknowledged with the contributor’s permission.

Help build measurable community value

Feedback and participation help HSKRC understand what is useful, what is missing and how the project can better serve the public.

Wherever you are in the world, you can use, contribute to or collaborate with HSKRC. Feedback from international users and local communities — including Greater Dandenong and Victoria — can help document the project’s real public and community impact.
