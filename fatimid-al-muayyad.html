Research dossier

Life in Shiraz

Al-Muʾayyad fi’l-Din Hibat Allah al-Shirazi was born around 1000 in Shiraz, in a family associated with the Ismaili daʿwa. Much of his earlier life is less securely documented than the career described in his surviving memoirs.

Research dossier

From Fars to Cairo

He first became influential within the political and religious circles of the Buyid period in Fars. Moving to Cairo, he worked in the service of the Fatimid imam-caliph al-Mustansir and became a leading scholar and figure in the central daʿwa organisation.

Research dossier

Works and source criticism

His Sira (memoirs) is significant as first-person testimony concerning religious and political networks of the eleventh century. His sermons and learned works also provide an important window into Fatimid Ismaili intellectual life. Modern studies by Verena Klemm and other scholars provide critical context.

Scholarly references differ over the exact death date (1078 or 1087). This page uses circa 1078 and flags the uncertainty rather than presenting both as certain.
