About · Research Standards · HSKRC

An independent research archive built around traceable evidence.

HSKRC is an independent digital research and knowledge archive dedicated to the documented study of Hassan Sabbah, Alamut, Nizari Ismaili history, and related cultural heritage.

No important claim without a source.

This principle guides the archive: significant historical, biographical, archaeological and heritage claims should remain traceable to identifiable evidence.

Editorial Principles

How HSKRC handles historical knowledge

01

Source-led research

Important claims are connected to scholarly publications, institutional records, heritage documentation or validated primary material whenever available.

02

Fact, interpretation and tradition

Legends and later traditions are not presented as established fact. Where they are historically relevant, they should be identified as traditions, claims or interpretations.

03

Multiple source types

The archive does not depend on a single reference work. Academic books, peer-reviewed research, UNESCO and ICOMOS documentation, specialist institutions and validated primary sources can all contribute.

04

Transparent uncertainty

When dates, attributions, functions or interpretations remain uncertain or disputed, HSKRC should preserve that uncertainty rather than manufacture precision.

05

Traceable source registry

Core references receive stable HSKRC source identifiers so knowledge pages and research articles can point back to a common source record.

06

Revision as evidence improves

Digital research records can be corrected, expanded and better sourced when stronger documentation becomes available.
