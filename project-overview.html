HSKRC in one page.

Hassan Sabbah Knowledge & Research Centre is an independent digital research platform that organises books, articles, researchers, historical knowledge and traceable sources around Hassan Sabbah, Alamut and Nizari Ismaili heritage.

Open Research Explorer Impact & Evidence Research Standards

The problem and the response

Specialised historical material is distributed across books, academic publications, institutional records and popular media. HSKRC reduces that fragmentation without presenting itself as a substitute for scholarship.

Problem

Students, researchers and general readers can struggle to distinguish documented history from legend, unsourced claims or isolated web pages, especially when relevant material is spread across languages and institutions.

Solution

HSKRC brings research records into one bilingual digital environment, connects topics to identifiable evidence, and provides a structured route from a historical subject to sources, researchers, articles and library records.

Technology, trust and accessibility

The platform uses lightweight web infrastructure while keeping research, privacy, copyright and accessibility requirements visible to users.

Technology

GitHub Pages provides deployment, Supabase provides structured data and authentication, and Cloudflare provides traffic analytics and automated-abuse protection. Public research pages remain readable without an account.

Data protection

Authentication and row-level access controls are handled through Supabase. Public forms use anti-bot protection, and HSKRC publishes privacy and terms information for users.

Accessibility

The interface includes responsive layouts, keyboard-friendly controls, labelled form elements, readable contrast and light/dark display modes, with bilingual navigation across core sections.

Research ethics

HSKRC does not train its own AI model. AI-assisted tools may support drafting, translation, coding or quality checks, while historical claims remain tied to identifiable scholarly or institutional sources.

Public value and SDG alignment

The project is designed as a free public research resource rather than a paywalled content product.

SDG 4 · Quality Education

HSKRC improves access to organised research material, bilingual educational content and traceable scholarly sources for students, researchers and general readers.

SDG 11.4 · Cultural Heritage

HSKRC documents and contextualises historical places, people, texts and heritage records connected to Alamut and Nizari Ismaili history, supporting awareness and digital preservation.

Evidence standard

Project quality is treated as an ongoing process rather than a marketing claim.

Traceability

Important historical claims should remain traceable to identifiable scholarly, institutional or primary sources whenever available.

Copyright

Local PDF access is enabled only when redistribution permission, an applicable licence or public-domain status is documented; otherwise official or lawful external access is used where available.

Corrections

Editorial policy provides a framework for corrections, source review and responsible handling of disputed or uncertain historical material.

Transparency

Researchers listed in the archive are represented because their work is relevant to source records; listing does not imply affiliation with or endorsement of HSKRC.
