Alamut as a historical centre

The fortress and the state

Encyclopaedia Iranica identifies Alamut as a mountain fortress in the central Alborz, in the Rudbar region. Hassan Sabbah seized the castle in 1090 and made it the centre of the Ismaili movement. UNESCO's 2026 World Heritage decision describes Alamut Castle as the headquarters of the Nizari Ismaili state founded by Hassan Sabbah in 1090.
