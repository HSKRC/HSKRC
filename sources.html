HSKRC Research Registry

Sources & References

A structured registry of scholarly books, primary texts, reference works, and institutional sources used to document Hassan Sabbah, Alamut, Nizari Ismaili history, and related cultural heritage.

Research principle: important historical claims should be traceable to an identifiable source. Where scholarship differs, HSKRC records the disagreement rather than hiding it.

HSKRC Source Method

The registry distinguishes modern scholarship, primary or edited historical texts, and reference works. Inclusion does not mean every statement in a source is accepted without comparison.

Rights & access: HSKRC does not redistribute copyrighted PDFs or commercial publications without permission or an applicable licence. Where redistribution is not authorised, the registry links to the official publisher or institutional access page.
