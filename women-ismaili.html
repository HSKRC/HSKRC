Fatimid Egypt · political leadership

Sitt al-Mulk

970–1023 CE

A Fatimid princess, influential court figure and entrepreneur. After the death of Imam-Caliph al-Hakim, she helped secure the succession of al-Zahir and served as regent and de facto ruler during the early years of his reign.

Yemen and Gujarat · ruler and hujja

Queen Arwa al-Sulayhi

c. 1048–1138 CE

The Sulayhid queen exercised political leadership in Yemen and was appointed hujja by Fatimid Imam-Caliph al-Mustansir. She supervised the da'wa in western India; the Ismaili community in Gujarat later developed into the Tayyibi Bohra community.

Iran and India · community leadership

Bibi Sarkara Maryam Khatun (Mata Salamat)

18th–19th century

After the killing of Imam Shah Khalil Allah in 1817, she petitioned the Qajar court for justice and helped protect her young son, Hasan Ali Shah (Aga Khan I). Later, as Pir Mata Salamat, she travelled to India to help resolve community disputes and gave sermons. The IIS article gives conflicting dates for her death in its heading and body; HSKRC leaves the exact year open pending source reconciliation.

South Asia · devotional literature

Sayyida Imam Begum

d. c. 1866 CE

One of the best-known female composers of ginans, the devotional poetry of South Asian Nizari Ismaili communities. IIS describes her as the tradition's only female composer; her songs continue to be recited in South Asian Nizari communities.

South Asia and East Africa · community leadership

Begum Um Habibeh (Yvonne Labrousse)

1906–2000

As the wife of the 48th Imam, Sultan Mahomed Shah Aga Khan III, Begum Um Habibeh took a public role in Ismaili community affairs and encouraged women's participation. A Harvard Divinity School study documents her influence alongside the community reforms of Aga Khan III.

Afghanistan · politics and women's rights

Farkhunda Zahra Naderi

born 1981

An Afghan former member of parliament and women's-rights advocate, later appointed senior adviser to the Afghan president on UN affairs. The Inter-Parliamentary Union records that her father led the Ismaili Shia community in Baghlan; her profile is included for her public service and role in Afghan Ismaili community life.

Global · institutional and social development

Princess Zahra Aga Khan

contemporary

A senior leader in the Aga Khan Development Network, with responsibilities spanning health, education and planning. She is Pro-Chancellor of Aga Khan University and serves on the boards of several AKDN institutions.

Canada and Eritrea · diplomacy and gender equality

Nahla Valji

contemporary

A Canadian UN leader with more than 25 years of work in sustainable development, peace and security, gender equality and human rights. The UN Secretary-General appointed her Resident Coordinator in Eritrea in 2024; an official Ismaili community feature includes her among Ismaili women serving at the United Nations.
