North Africa and the early capitals

The Fatimid state arose in 909 in Ifriqiya. The purpose-built coastal capital al-Mahdiyya was established under al-Mahdi around 920 and included a palace complex and naval facilities. Al-Mansur founded al-Mansuriyya, near Qayrawan, in the middle of the tenth century. These precedents are important in the history of Fatimid urban planning.

The foundation of Cairo

In 969 Jawhar al-Siqilli entered Egypt and founded a planned capital. Al-Muʿizz himself arrived in 973. Cairo and the older settlement of Fustat were related but distinct urban centres. Later dynasties expanded the metropolis beyond the Fatimid core.
