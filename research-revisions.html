This log records completed editorial changes. It is separate from independent peer review and from acceptance decisions in the live evidence register.

2026-10-30 · Citation and evidence update

  • 88 historical, biographical, memorial and article pages now carry publication records, evidence limitations, citation exports and links to version history.
  • The preservation-of-knowledge article was revised in English and Dari with an abstract, method, source-specific citations and a distinction between catalogue descriptions and direct manuscript examination.

Editing and implementation used AI assistance. This release does not appoint an academic reviewer, change a queued record into an accepted record, or establish university affiliation.

How to cite and verify

Use the publication record below a page’s title. Record the accessed date and section. RIS and CSL-JSON exports identify the HSKRC page, not its underlying books. Follow individual references for evidence. The record revision date describes this edition, not the date of every historical claim’s verification.
