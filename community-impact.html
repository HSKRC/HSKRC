Community Impact

HSKRC is an independent digital cultural and research project created to preserve, organise and make historical knowledge easier to discover. Its public-facing work combines a digital library, research articles, source-led knowledge pages and bilingual access.

Connection to Greater Dandenong

HSKRC is being developed in Victoria with a community-facing goal: to make cultural and historical knowledge easier to access, including for diverse communities in and around Greater Dandenong.

HSKRC does not claim community impact that has not yet been measured. As the project grows, local participation, feedback, usage and partnerships will be documented here with verifiable evidence.
