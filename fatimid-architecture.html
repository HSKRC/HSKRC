Architecture as evidence

Fatimid Cairo is part of the UNESCO-listed Historic Cairo property. Mosques, carved facades, walls and gates provide tangible evidence of several stages of the city’s history. Surviving structures include later rebuilding and restoration; the date of the first foundation is not necessarily the date of all fabric seen today.

Research collection

Research and image policy

Every heritage photograph should identify the building, photographer, source and licence. Modern photographs are not evidence that an entire structure remains unchanged since the Fatimid period.
