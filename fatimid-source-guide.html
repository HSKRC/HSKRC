How HSKRC evaluates evidence

  1. Separate the original author and date of a text from the editor and modern publication year.
  2. Identify whether the work is a contemporary record, a later chronicle, or modern analysis.
  3. Do not treat partisan claims, later legends or reconstructed portraits as verified historical facts.
  4. Record stable publisher URLs, bibliographic details, author, edition and known limitations.
