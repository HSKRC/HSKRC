Qosṭinlār Fortification

A source-led research node for Qosṭinlār, component 1770-005 of the UNESCO World Heritage property Alamūt Castle and Related Fortifications.

1770-005Qazvin ProvinceWorld Heritage · 2026
Verified Profile

Official component data

Fifth component of the serial property

UNESCO lists Qosṭinlār as one of six fortifications associated with Alamut Castle. Together, the seven components form a coordinated defensive system across the Alamut Valley. [HSKRC-SRC-0009] [HSKRC-SRC-0010]

Architecture & Position

A road-controlling fortified plateau

Strategic setting

ICOMOS records Qosṭinlār at about 1,812 metres above sea level, southwest of Alamut. Its position overlooks the route linking Qazvin and Alamut and the Šāhrud Valley, supporting strategic control and visual communication with neighbouring fortifications. [HSKRC-SRC-0012]

Defensive layout

The ICOMOS evaluation describes a rectangular plateau with a central keep, ancillary structures on the southern slope, a perimeter wall and thirteen regularly spaced towers connected by corridors. It records rubble stone, dressed stone and gypsum mortar among the construction materials. [HSKRC-SRC-0012]

Function & Evidence

Surveillance, defence and communication

Although partially destroyed, the surviving remains are interpreted in the ICOMOS evaluation as evidence of Qosṭinlār's role in surveillance, defence and control of strategic routes. At the scale of the entire serial property, UNESCO emphasises intervisibility and functional relationships among the fortifications as attributes of the network's integrity. [HSKRC-SRC-0012] [HSKRC-SRC-0010]

HSKRC separates component-specific evidence from network-wide claims.
World Heritage Context

Part of the Nizari fortified landscape

Alamūt Castle and Related Fortifications was inscribed in 2026 under criterion (iii). UNESCO describes the ensemble as rare testimony to the Nezāri Ismaʿili cultural tradition that flourished in the Alborz Mountains from the eleventh to the thirteenth centuries, integrating defence, administration, scholarship and community life. [HSKRC-SRC-0010]
