Programme in development · 2026–2027

Sustainability & Global Digital Heritage Learning

HSKRC is developing a free, instructor-led online education programme connecting cultural heritage preservation, digital access to knowledge and social and environmental sustainability. The programme is intended to serve learners in Afghanistan and African countries.

Status: Planning stage. Classes, enrolment, partnerships, participant numbers and outcomes have not yet been confirmed. This is an independent HSKRC initiative, not an IFLA-endorsed or award-winning programme.

Learning pathways

Afghanistan · Dari

Planned free online classes in Dari, including cultural heritage documentation, digital archiving, water and environmental stewardship, and community knowledge preservation.

African countries and other regions · English

Planned free online classes in English on digital heritage, sustainable libraries, environmental literacy and equitable access to trustworthy information.

Teaching will be delivered by HSKRC's designated instructor. Dates and enrolment instructions will be published when confirmed.

Our objectives

  • Improve inclusive access to educational and heritage resources.
  • Build practical skills in responsible digital preservation.
  • Connect cultural knowledge with environmental and social sustainability.
  • Encourage local participation and respectful documentation of community knowledge.

Delivery and accountability

HSKRC will coordinate the curriculum, safeguarding and consent practices, registration and attendance records, and educational feedback. The instructor will lead sessions. Any public reporting will use aggregated or consented information, not personal details of learners.

Impact and evidence

Once classes begin, this page will report verified figures for sessions held, countries represented, attendance, learning feedback, accessible resources produced and relevant sustainability outcomes. No projected targets will be presented as achieved results.

Related global framework

This programme is being designed with reference to sustainability and library-sector principles, including the IFLA Green Library Award. Mentioning IFLA does not imply affiliation, eligibility, endorsement or an award.
